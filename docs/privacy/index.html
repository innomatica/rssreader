Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026.09.17

This privacy policy applies to the RSS Reader mobile application (herein referred to as "the Application") for mobile devices, which was created by innomatic (herein referred to as "the Developer") as a Free and Open Source service.

1. Information Collection and Use

The Application does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or user data of any kind.

Because we do not collect any information, we do not use, process, or transfer your personal data to any third parties.

2. Permissions and Device Data

The Application operates entirely locally on your device. It does not request or require access to sensitive device permissions (such as your location, camera, microphone, or contacts) that could be used to track your behavior or identify you.

3. Third-Party Access

The Application does not integrate with any third-party services, analytics software, or advertising networks that collect or track user data.

4. Children’s Privacy

The Developer does not use the Application to knowingly solicit data from or market to children under the age of 13. Since the Application does not collect any data, no information from children is ever gathered or stored.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

The Developer may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. The Developer will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, please do not hesitate to contact the Developer at:

  • Email: info.innomatic@gmail.com
  • Website: https://innomatic.ca
